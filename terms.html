PengeFlyt Terms of Use

PengeFlyt is a private, non-commercial household finance application intended solely for personal use by its owner.

The application may retrieve read-only bank account information through Enable Banking, including transactions and balances.

PengeFlyt does not provide banking services, payment initiation, financial advice, or services to third parties.

The application is used at the owner's own responsibility for personal financial overview and planning.

This website exists only to provide information and a redirect destination for bank authorization.
