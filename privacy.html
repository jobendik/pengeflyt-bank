PengeFlyt Privacy Notice

PengeFlyt is a private household finance application used by its owner for personal financial management.

The application may connect to bank accounts through Enable Banking in order to retrieve read-only account information, including transactions and balances.

PengeFlyt does not initiate payments or bank transfers.

Bank credentials and authorization information are handled locally by PengeFlyt and are not stored on this website.

This website does not collect, store, or process bank data, personal financial data, cookies, analytics, or tracking information.

Any bank data retrieved through Enable Banking is used only for the owner's personal household finance management.
